# Cross-Cultural Considerations

## Challenge

Similarity perception and face recognition systems can present biases across ethnicities, ages, genders, and cultures.

## Our Approach

- Use diverse and representative datasets for metric validation
- Document performance variations across demographic groups
- Encourage global participation to identify and mitigate biases
- Thresholds should be adaptable by region when justified by local studies

## Goal

Create a framework that is **technically robust** while remaining **culturally sensitive** and fair across different populations.
