# Benchmarks and References

## Key Papers and Studies

- ArcFace: Additive Angular Margin Loss for Deep Face Recognition (2019)
- LPIPS: The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Deep Features as a Perceptual Metric (2018)
- Deepfake Detection Benchmarks (FaceForensics++, DFDC, Celeb-DF)
- Studies on Human Detection Performance on Deepfakes (2024-2026)

## Current Benchmark Status

- Face recognition systems achieve >99% accuracy on clean data, but drop significantly on adversarial examples.
- Human accuracy on high-quality deepfakes is often between 50-75%.

This folder will be updated with new relevant research as the community contributes.
