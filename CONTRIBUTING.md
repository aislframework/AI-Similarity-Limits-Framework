# Contributing Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the AI-Similarity-Limits-Framework.

## How to Contribute

1. **Discuss first**: Open an Issue to discuss major changes or new ideas.
2. **Fork & Pull Request**: Make changes in your fork and submit a PR.
3. **Be clear**: Provide technical justification for any proposed changes to thresholds or metrics.

## Types of Contributions

- Improving technical documents
- Adding new research references
- Proposing changes to thresholds
- Translating documents
- Creating examples and case studies
- Governance and process suggestions

## Code of Conduct

Please follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). We expect respectful and constructive dialogue.

## Recognition

All contributors will be credited in [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md).
