# Original Framework (v0.1)

This document presents the initial proposal for **Objective Similarity Limits** for generative AI and deepfake regulation.

## Thresholds

| Similarity Level | Classification | Requirements |
|------------------|----------------|--------------|
| **< 70%** | Generic Person | Free use. No restrictions. Considered original creation. |
| **70-85%** | Noticeable Similarity | Allowed, but **mandatory disclaimer** required ("This content was AI-generated and resembles a real person"). |
| **85-95%** | High Similarity | Requires **explicit consent** from the person depicted or their legal representative. |
| **> 95%** | Very High Similarity / Digital Replica | Considered a serious violation if used without authorization, especially in harmful contexts (may constitute crime). |

## Core Principles

- **Proportionality**: Higher similarity = higher responsibility
- **Context Matters**: Exceptions should exist for satire, journalism, education, and public interest (to be detailed in `exceptions.md`)
- **Technical Measurability**: Thresholds should be based on standardized, reproducible metrics
- **Adaptability**: Countries can adjust thresholds according to local laws and cultural values

This framework is **open-source** and designed to be improved through community consensus.
