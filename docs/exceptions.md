# Exceptions and Special Cases

The framework must balance protection with fundamental rights. This document lists recommended exceptions.

## Allowed Exceptions (even in higher similarity bands)

- **Satire and Parody**: Protected under freedom of expression.
- **Artistic and Creative Works**: Movies, theater, digital art, etc.
- **Journalism and Public Interest**: When there is clear news value.
- **Education and Research**: Academic use with proper citation.
- **Political Speech**: With limitations (especially during elections).
- **Historical or Documentary Use**: With context.

## Requirements for Exceptions

- Must include clear disclosure that the content is AI-generated.
- Cannot be used for commercial purposes without consent.
- Cannot be used to create non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII).

All exceptions must be judged under a **proportionality test**.
